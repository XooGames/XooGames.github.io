Privacy Policy

Effective date: 12 November 2026 · Last updated: 16 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored and shared when you play Spirito or Ripple Bloom, mobile games published under the name XOO Games. We aim to handle player information responsibly and transparently.

Developer: XOO Games
Contact: xoogamesco@gmail.com
Studio: Independent Game Developer

XOO Games is the public developer name used by an individual developer operating a personal Google Play developer account. References to “we”, “us” or “our” mean the developer operating under the XOO Games name. “The games” means Spirito and Ripple Bloom. Where practices differ, we say which game they apply to.

1. Introduction

By downloading or using Spirito or Ripple Bloom, you acknowledge that you have read this Privacy Policy. It describes the information practices of the games and their features.

2. Information We Handle

Spirito

  • Player account information: a player identifier generated by the authentication service, plus authentication and account-linking information (guest accounts and linked accounts, where supported).
  • Player profile: username or display name, country selection, avatar, and profile and gameplay statistics.
  • Gameplay information: best and last-run score, total collectibles, total playtime, leaderboard scores and rankings.
  • Purchase information: if you buy a product such as Remove Ads, the app-store billing provider processes the transaction. We may receive purchase and entitlement information to confirm the feature is available to your account. We do not intend to collect or store your full payment card details.

Ripple Bloom

  • Ripple Bloom has no accounts or sign-in, and we do not ask for your name, email address or location.
  • The following is saved only on your device: best score, sound and volume settings, hint count and refill timers, daily goal and score-milestone progress, and whether you have seen the tutorial. We do not receive this information.

Technical information (both games)

The games and their service providers may process technical information needed to operate, secure, troubleshoot and improve the games, and to show and measure ads. The exact information depends on the services enabled in the version you use.

3. How We Use Information

  • Create and maintain player accounts and synchronize profiles and progress (Spirito).
  • Display leaderboard scores and rankings (Spirito).
  • Provide gameplay features, rewards and purchased entitlements.
  • Show, measure and protect advertisements through our advertising partners.
  • Respond to support requests and detect abuse, cheating, fraud and technical problems.
  • Maintain and improve performance and reliability, and meet legal and platform requirements.

4. Third-Party Services

The games use third-party services that process information under their own privacy policies. We do not control their independent practices.

Spirito

  • Unity Authentication, Cloud Save and Leaderboards: guest sign-in, account linking, saving player data, and scores and rankings.
  • Google Play services and Google Play Billing: Android platform features and in-app purchases.
  • Advertising providers: if advertising is enabled, partners may process device, advertising or interaction information to deliver, measure and protect ads.

Ripple Bloom

  • Google AdMob: optional rewarded ads and ads between games. See how Google uses data from apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google's consent form: shown in regions where the law requires consent for ads, such as the EEA and UK.
  • The app store you downloaded from (for example Google Play or Samsung Galaxy Store), under its own policies.
  • Game portals: if you play on a portal such as CrazyGames or YouTube Playables, that platform serves its own ads and handles its own data under its own privacy policy.

5. Advertising

Spirito: may display advertisements, including optional rewarded ads, through advertising service providers. Before a Remove Ads purchase, providers may process device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP address, ad interaction and diagnostic information, depending on the provider, your device settings and consent requirements. If you successfully buy Remove Ads, it is intended to disable all ads in Spirito, including optional rewarded ads.

Ripple Bloom: shows optional rewarded ads (to earn a hint or continue a game) and ads between games. Ads are requested as child-directed, so they are not personalized and the advertising ID is not used for them. Google AdMob may still process limited technical information, such as IP address and device and app information, to deliver, measure and protect ads. Ripple Bloom does not currently sell any product that removes ads; if that changes, we will update this policy.

Where required, we provide appropriate disclosures and consent choices before enabling the relevant advertising processing.

6. In-App Purchases

Spirito offers a Remove Ads purchase. Purchases are processed by Google Play or the applicable platform billing service, which may give us information confirming a purchase so we can provide the feature. For billing questions, refunds or payment-method issues, you may also need to contact Google Play under its policies. Ripple Bloom does not currently offer in-app purchases.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell players' personal information. Information may be shared with service providers when reasonably necessary to operate the games, including authentication, cloud saving, leaderboards, advertising, purchase processing, security and technical support. It may also be disclosed when required by law, to respond to valid legal requests, or to protect the rights, safety and security of players and the games.

8. Data Storage and Retention

Spirito player information may be stored by the service providers it uses. Ripple Bloom progress is stored on your device only. We keep information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the games, maintain accounts and purchases, resolve disputes, prevent abuse, or meet legal obligations. Retention periods may differ between providers and categories of information.

9. Account and Data Deletion

Spirito

You may request deletion of your Spirito account and associated player data by emailing:

Email: xoogamesco@gmail.com
Subject: Spirito – Account and Data Deletion Request

Please provide the player ID or username needed to identify your account. Do not send passwords or payment card details. We may request reasonable information to verify that you control the account. After verification, we will delete associated data that we are not required or permitted to retain; certain records may be kept for legal, security, fraud-prevention or transaction-related reasons. We aim to respond within 30 days and will explain if more time is reasonably needed. Deletion may permanently remove saved progress, profile information and leaderboard-related data, and does not automatically cancel payments managed by Google Play. See also our Account Deletion page.

Ripple Bloom

Ripple Bloom stores no account data on our servers. Uninstalling the game or clearing its app data deletes everything it saved. Information held by advertising providers can be managed through your device's advertising settings and Google's ad settings. You can also email us with any request.

10. Children's Privacy

Ripple Bloom

Ripple Bloom is a calm, family-friendly puzzle with no violence, no chat and no contact between players. It is designed to help players of all ages, including young children playing with a parent or guardian, practise spatial reasoning and problem solving as they learn to fit blocks onto the ripple grid. It has no accounts or sign-in, and we do not ask for or knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads in Ripple Bloom are requested as child-directed: they are not personalized, are limited to general-audience content, and the advertising ID is not used for these requests.

Spirito

Spirito is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, contact xoogamesco@gmail.com so we can review the matter and take appropriate action.

11. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled through the games and their service providers. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. Please protect access to your device and any linked accounts.

12. International Processing

Some service providers may process information on servers outside your country, where it may be subject to the laws of those locations.

13. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of or deletion of certain personal information. You may also manage advertising preferences through your device settings or the choices shown by the advertising provider. For privacy requests, contact xoogamesco@gmail.com.

14. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when the games, their features, service providers or legal requirements change. The updated version will be published on this page and the “Last updated” date will be revised.
