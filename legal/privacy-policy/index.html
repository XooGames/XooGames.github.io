Privacy Policy

Effective date: 12 November 2026 · Last updated: 12 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored and shared when you play Glow Spirit, a mobile game published under the name XOO Games. We aim to handle player information responsibly and transparently.

Developer: XOO Games
Contact: xoogamesco@gmail.com
Studio: Independent Game Developer

XOO Games is the public developer name used by an individual developer operating a personal Google Play developer account. References to “we”, “us” or “our” in this policy mean the developer operating under the XOO Games name.

1. Introduction

By downloading or using Glow Spirit, you acknowledge that you have read this Privacy Policy. This policy describes the information practices associated with the game and its available features.

2. Information We Handle

Depending on the features you use, Glow Spirit and its service providers may handle the following categories of information.

Player account information
  • A player identifier generated by the authentication service.
  • Authentication and account-linking information needed to maintain your player account.
  • Guest-account information and information associated with a linked account, where supported.
Player profile information
  • Username or display name.
  • Country selection or country code.
  • Selected avatar.
  • Profile and gameplay statistics.
Gameplay information
  • Best score and last-run score.
  • Total collectibles.
  • Total playtime.
  • Leaderboard scores and ranking information.
Purchase information

If you purchase an in-game product, such as Remove Ads, the relevant app-store billing provider processes the transaction. We may receive purchase and entitlement information needed to confirm that the purchased feature is available to your account. We do not intend to collect or store your full payment card details.

Technical information

The game and its service providers may process technical information needed to operate, secure, troubleshoot and improve the game. The exact information depends on the services and software development kits enabled in the released version.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Create and maintain player accounts.
  • Save and synchronize player profiles and game progress.
  • Display leaderboard scores and player rankings.
  • Provide gameplay features and purchased entitlements.
  • Support account linking and account recovery where available.
  • Respond to support requests.
  • Detect abuse, cheating, fraud and technical problems.
  • Maintain and improve game performance and reliability.
  • Meet applicable legal and platform requirements.

4. Third-Party Services

Glow Spirit may use third-party services to provide its features. These services process information under their own privacy policies and terms. Depending on the released version, these may include:

  • Unity Authentication: to provide guest sign-in and supported account authentication or linking.
  • Unity Cloud Save: to store and retrieve player profile information and game progress.
  • Unity Leaderboards: to submit and display player scores and rankings.
  • Google Play services and Google Play Billing: to support Android platform features and in-app purchases.
  • Advertising providers: if advertising is enabled, advertising partners may process device, advertising or interaction information to deliver, measure and protect advertisements.

Third-party providers may process information in accordance with their own policies. We do not control their independent privacy practices. The actual providers used in the released version should be identified in this policy.

5. Advertising

Glow Spirit may display advertisements, including optional rewarded advertisements, through advertising service providers.

Before a Remove Ads purchase, advertising providers may process information such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP address, advertisement interaction information and diagnostic information. The information processed depends on the provider, your device settings and applicable consent requirements.

Remove Ads: If you successfully purchase the Remove Ads product, the purchase is intended to disable all advertisements in Glow Spirit, including regular advertisements and optional rewarded advertisements.

Where required, we will provide appropriate privacy disclosures and consent choices before enabling relevant advertising processing.

6. In-App Purchases

Glow Spirit may offer in-app purchases, including a Remove Ads purchase.

Purchases are processed through Google Play or the applicable platform billing service. The platform may provide us with information confirming a transaction or purchase entitlement so that we can provide the purchased feature.

For billing questions, refunds or payment-method issues, users may also need to contact Google Play in accordance with its policies.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell players' personal information.

Information may be shared with service providers when reasonably necessary to operate the game, including authentication, cloud saving, leaderboards, advertising, purchase processing, security and technical support.

Information may also be disclosed when required by law, to respond to valid legal requests, or to protect the rights, safety and security of players and the game.

8. Data Storage and Retention

Some player information may be stored by the service providers used by Glow Spirit.

We retain information for as long as it is reasonably necessary to provide the game, maintain player accounts and purchases, resolve disputes, prevent abuse, or meet legal obligations. Retention periods may differ between service providers and categories of information.

9. Account and Data Deletion

You may request deletion of your Glow Spirit account and associated player data by emailing:

Email: xoogamesco@gmail.com
Subject: Glow Spirit – Account and Data Deletion Request

Please provide the player ID or username needed to identify your account. Do not send passwords or payment card details. We may request reasonable information to verify that you control the account.

After verification, we will process the request and delete associated data that we are not required or permitted to retain. Certain records may need to be retained for legal, security, fraud-prevention or transaction-related reasons.

We aim to respond to deletion requests within 30 days. If additional time is reasonably necessary, we will explain this to you.

Account deletion may permanently remove saved progress, profile information and leaderboard-related data associated with the account. It may not automatically cancel subscriptions or other payments managed by Google Play.

You can also visit our Account Deletion page for deletion instructions.

10. Children's Privacy

Glow Spirit is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, please contact us at xoogamesco@gmail.com so that we can review the matter and take appropriate action.

The game's target age group and any child-directed advertising settings must be accurately reflected in its Google Play listing and advertising configuration.

11. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled through the game and its service providers. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Players should protect access to their devices and linked accounts.

12. International Processing

Some service providers may process information on servers located outside your country. Where this occurs, information may be subject to the laws applicable in those locations.

13. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of or deletion of certain personal information.

You may also be able to manage advertising preferences through your device settings or choices presented by the advertising provider.

For privacy requests, contact xoogamesco@gmail.com.
